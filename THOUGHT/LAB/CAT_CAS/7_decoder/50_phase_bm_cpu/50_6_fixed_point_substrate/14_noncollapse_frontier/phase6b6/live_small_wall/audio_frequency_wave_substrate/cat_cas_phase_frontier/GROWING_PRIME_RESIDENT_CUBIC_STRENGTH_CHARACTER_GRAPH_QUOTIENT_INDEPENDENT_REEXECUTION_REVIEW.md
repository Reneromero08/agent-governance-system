## Pareto obstruction and accounting

The result changes representation, not the computational resource boundary.
The factor graph avoids resident q² amplitude materialization only by moving
the cost into final contraction: cache-free projection grows as
`8*q^(2*d)`. An identical classical graph executes the same recurrence with
the same state and work.

The work-reduced exact Rader/NTT transfer remains the second matched Pareto
point. It retains `2*q²` field cells plus counted linear scratch, and every
declared boundary agrees with the independent dense recurrence. The underlying
Rader implementation and resource recurrence were independently qualified in
the immediately preceding resident-port package; this review rechecks its
semantic boundary, `2*q²` resident count, absence of retained kernel cache,
and exactness-bound assertion, but does not claim a new independent Rader
implementation.

The production runner deliberately materializes the expanded classical/Rader
comparison after graph projection. Therefore “no q² amplitude field” applies
to the accepted graph algorithm and carrier, not to the whole qualification
process. Python frames and objects, allocator behavior, interpreter state,
native libraries, and whole-process peaks are excluded from material-cell
counts.

## Ceiling

The ceiling is the nine declared safe-prime pairs, the listed primary and
alternate depths, two fibers, direct-process finite-field software, and
auxiliary NTT modulus 998,244,353. The result establishes neither 3q amplitude
state closure nor fixed work across growing depth. Runtime factors are public
reproducible fixtures and are not machine-hidden.

It does not establish CATVM custody, a distinct phase resource, computational
advantage, a Small Wall crossing, physical waveform or silicon execution,
replacement of physical bits with pi, or unbounded catalytic computation.
