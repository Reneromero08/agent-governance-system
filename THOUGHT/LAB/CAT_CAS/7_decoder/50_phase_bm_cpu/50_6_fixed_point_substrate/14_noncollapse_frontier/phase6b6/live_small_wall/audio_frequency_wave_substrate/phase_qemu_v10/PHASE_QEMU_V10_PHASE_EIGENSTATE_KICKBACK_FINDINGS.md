## Exact qutrit mechanism

Every claim-bearing equality is evaluated in

```text
Q(omega),  omega^2 + omega + 1 = 0.
```

An element is represented as `a+b omega` with exact rational coefficients.
Multiplication reduces `omega^2` to `-1-omega`; conjugation maps `omega` to
`omega^2`. Normalized character and Bell amplitudes would require square
roots, so the executables instead use their exact projectors, whose entries
remain in `Q(omega)`. No float, trigonometric approximation, tolerance, or
rounded comparison participates in a decision.

With

```text
X_a |y> = |y+a mod 3>
|chi_k> = (1/sqrt(3)) sum_y omega^(-k y) |y>
P_k[y,z] = omega^(k(z-y))/3,
```

production proves for `k=1` that

```text
X_a |chi_1> = omega^a |chi_1>
X_a P_1 X_a^dagger = P_1
Tr(P_1) = 1
P_1^2 = P_1
P_1^dagger = P_1.
```

The accepted coherent query is an actual 54-row basis permutation,

```text
O_f |x,y,s,r> = |x,y+f(x) mod 3,s,r>,
```

applied to all `54^2=2916` density-matrix positions. It is not replaced on
the accepted path by the already-known phase formula. Exact reconstruction
then verifies

```text
O_f (rho_C tensor P_1 tensor Phi_3) O_f^dagger
  = (D_f rho_C D_f^dagger) tensor P_1 tensor Phi_3,

D_f = diag_x omega^f(x).
```

Thus the client changes while the secret-independent target-character
projector and the inert qutrit Bell sentinel return and completely factorize.

## Controls that prevent a marginal-only reading

The computational-basis control replaces `P_1` by `|0><0|` and executes both
oracle permutations. For A and B, the client branches reach distinct target
basis states, client purity falls to `1/2`, the pure joint state is entangled,
and the target fails to return. The accepted result therefore depends on the
phase-eigenstate law rather than a ceremonial untouched target.

For a character mixture



client/carrier state remains correlated, and the joint state does not return.

environment holds two orthogonal branch records, the client fully dephases,

evidence of a unique phase resource.

```text
<p_f|p_g><psi|phi>
```

## Scientific basis

The controlled-eigenstate phase mechanism and its relation to phase estimation
follow Cleve, Ekert, Macchiavello, and Mosca,
[“Quantum Algorithms Revisited,” arXiv:quant-ph/9708016](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9708016).
The exact reusable-program orthogonality requirement is the deterministic
programming boundary of Nielsen and Chuang,
[“Programmable quantum gate arrays,” arXiv:quant-ph/9703032](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9703032).
These papers support the algebraic ingredients, not M268's software-custody
claim or any hardware promotion.

The prospective query-complexity target is Aaronson and Ambainis,
[“Forrelation: A Problem that Optimally Separates Quantum from Classical Computing,” arXiv:1411.5729](https://arxiv.org/abs/1411.5729).
That primary result does not turn the public two-entry tables used here into a
Forrelation oracle.

The finite-energy warning for oscillator encodings is consistent with
Gottesman, Kitaev, and Preskill,
[“Encoding a qubit in an oscillator,” arXiv:quant-ph/0008040](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0008040):
an ideal oscillator-code construction is not free physical preparation and
finite-energy approximation, nonlinear preparation, noise, and certification
must be exposed. Heeres et al.,
[“Cavity State Manipulation Using Photon-Number Selective Phase Gates,” arXiv:1503.01496](https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.01496),
experimentally support two narrower ingredients: photon-number-selective phase
control through an off-resonantly coupled qubit and preparation of a
one-photon Fock state. Teoh et al.,
[“Dual-rail encoding with superconducting cavities,” arXiv:2212.12077](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.12077),
support the complementary circuit-QED ingredient of encoding a qubit in the
single-photon subspace of two superconducting microwave cavities. None of
these papers establishes the proposed Phase-QEMU transaction, oracle access
law, restoration boundary, or resource advantage.

## V11 physical candidate and mandatory device gate

The next concrete physical candidate is a finite-energy dual-rail,
one-photon circuit-QED number-eigenspace dispersive-kickback experiment. The
logical carrier would occupy the fixed total-number-one subspace of two cavity
rails, with explicitly measured preparation, loss/leakage, phase error,
dispersive coupling, pulse energy, calibration, certification, latency,
bandwidth, environmental record, and post-transaction return. The coherent
oracle-generation law, secrecy/access enforcement, equal-interface
comparator, and every reset or replacement path must be physically explicit.

This candidate is a proposal assembled from supported ingredients, not a
claim that the required oracle or catalytic return has been demonstrated. A
fixed number eigenspace can supply phase kickback, but one photon is finite
energy rather than free; dual-rail loss, ancilla records, dispersive errors,
and preparation/rematerialization remain charged. Exact logical return cannot
be inferred from a number marginal or from a successful phase gate alone.

V11 must be an actual swappable common Phase-QEMU backend/device, not another
Python-only package, before it can be considered for architecture promotion.
It must preserve the common guest-visible interface while swapping the
backend and must demonstrate lifecycle, custody, guest boundary ordering,
restoration before release, same-backing reuse, and snapshot/reload plus
migration-sham lineage. Failure of the physical mechanism may still kill it
without promotion; survival of the mechanism merely makes it eligible to run
that architecture gate.
