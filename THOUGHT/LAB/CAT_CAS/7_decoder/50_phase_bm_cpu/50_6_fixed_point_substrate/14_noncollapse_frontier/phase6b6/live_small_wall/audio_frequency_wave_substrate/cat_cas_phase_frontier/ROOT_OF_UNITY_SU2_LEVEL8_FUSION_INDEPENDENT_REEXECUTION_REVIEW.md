## Reconstructed mechanism

The production path replaces the unbounded continuous-`SU(2)` representation
ring with the level-8 Jones-Wenzl quotient over exact `Q(zeta_40)`.  Its nine
simple-object coordinates obey
`X_1 X_j=X_(j-1)+X_(j+1)` with `X_9=0`.  Public topological twists multiply
simple object `j` by powers of `zeta_40^(j(j+2))`.  Twist and truncated
fundamental fusion do not commute.

One nine-cell carrier and one nine-cell inverse scratch backing remain bound
to an unresolved typed port.  A two-integer `(depth,family)` descriptor
derives every operation by index; no depth-sized operation list or inverse
history is retained.  Only the final exact quantum-dimension character is
projected.  This is direct-process typed custody, not CATVM isolation or an
enforced no-smuggle boundary.

## Restoration, controls and resources

Production reverses each fusion by rematerializing nine tridiagonal pivots on
the actual scratch backing, solving on the actual carrier backing, then
recomputing and subtracting the pivots in reverse until scratch is exactly
zero.  Reverse root twists then restore the exact source.  The carrier and
scratch backing identities survive; restored reuse agrees with a fresh
carrier at restoration generation two without snapshot reload.

Wrong owner, wrong operation type, premature projection, missing inverse,
reordered inverse, null carrier, wrong inverse factor and semantic fusion
perturbation controls discriminate.  Swapping a twist/fusion pair changes
both the independent quotient polynomial and final boundary.

At depth 128 the forward carrier remains nine field cells but grows from a
290-bit initial payload to 15,330 bits; maximum numerator width is 108 bits
and the final boundary carries 1,761 bits.  The full inverse lifecycle reaches
18,083 carrier bits, 1,593 pivot-scratch bits and 19,674 combined bits.  It
rematerializes and uncomputes 1,152 pivot cells, uses 4,224 high-level field
inversions, and retains zero pivot-plan or inverse-history entries.  The
public program retains two descriptor integers, materializes zero operation
records and derives 512 forward/inverse operation visits.  The independent
ordinary-polynomial state has nine cells carrying 15,660 bits.

Exact field-coordinate payload, public descriptors, commitment visits and
high-level field operations are counted.  Public index arithmetic, rational
scalar suboperations, transient Python objects, allocator/interpreter state,
hash byte traffic, serialization, timing and whole-process peaks are excluded,
not zero.
