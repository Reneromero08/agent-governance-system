# Reflection-Graded Stokes BCH Phase Format

## Exact grading

Both public quadratic generators are even under the reflection
`y -> -y`. For reflection parities `p` and `q`, every term of the Stokes
Lie-Poisson bracket has parity:

```text
p + q + 1 mod 2
```

Therefore a right-nested Lie word of length `n` has predeclared `y` parity
`n - 1 mod 2`. This is derived from public topology before execution.

## Carrier quotient

The phase carrier compiles only monomials satisfying both the established
total-degree parity and the reflection grade. The same reduced basis is used
for declared final BCH components and reusable rematerialization scratch.
Character-phase addition, public scalar index permutation, topology-derived
word rematerialization, actual inverse restoration, and restored-carrier
reuse are unchanged.

The exact verifier checks every retained dual-prime cell against the
independent rational BCH oracle. It also checks every excluded monomial in
the full parity basis and requires its rational coefficient to be exactly
zero. No excluded coefficient is decoded from the phase carrier because no
excluded phase cell exists.

## Boundary and ceiling

The final boundary remains the complete BCH coefficient signature through
word grade six, now expressed on its exact reflection sector. Snapshot sham,
missing inverse, wrong inverse, swapped noncommuting order, scratch
projection denial, and null-carrier controls are retained.

The claim is bounded software evidence. The quotient is not fixed-rank
closure, does not prove an arbitrary representation lower bound or unbounded
rank growth, and does not establish a distinct phase resource, advantage,
Small Wall crossing, or physical waveform execution.
