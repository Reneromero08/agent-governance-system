## Reconstructed mechanism

The accepted machine no longer stores the cubic-deformed operator as a
quadratic Weyl-component chart. It stores `R` actual two-fiber source-state
columns in `2*q*R` field cells and applies the public cubic/Weil word
factorwise. Cubic diagonals, Gaussian kernels, and the final two-fiber map act
directly on the resident columns. Only four declared entries are combined at
the final scalar boundary. The inverse factors are rematerialized from the
public word; no inverse history or snapshot is retained.

The independent oracle imports neither production nor either predecessor. It
reconstructs the safe-prime fields, projective Gaussian inverses, public
descriptor schedule, factorwise bundle recurrence, source-streamed q-vector
boundary, rank calculation, reverse sequence, controls, and reuse using
separate tuple/list code. Production JSON is used only as a comparison target.

## Executed evidence

- All eleven declared cases were independently reconstructed. Primary depth-2
  cases cover q values 5, 11, 23, 29, 41, 53, 83, 89, and 113; q=113 also runs
  primary depth 4, and q=41 runs alternate depth 3.
- Source rank is 10 at q=5 and 22 elsewhere. Every observed action span equals
  the declared rank. Full two-fiber basis bundles were therefore executed at
  q=5 and q=11; larger orders execute rank 22, not their full `2*q` basis.
- Every boundary, complete bundle commitment, work tuple, resident/temporary
  cell count, source-streamed baseline result, exact restoration state, and
  backing-identity assertion agrees with production.
- Separate dense factor multiplication matches the complete full-basis bundle
  at q=5 depth 2 and q=11 depth 1. Both dense operators have full ranks 10 and
  22 respectively.
- Missing, wrong, and reordered inverses fail; module reordering changes the
  boundary; premature projection, invalid family/depth, zero rank, and
  oversize rank are rejected.
- Primary depth 1 followed by alternate depth 2 on the same q=23 rank-22
  backing reaches generation two, restores the exact payload after both uses,
  and matches a fresh second transaction without a snapshot.

## Resource law, comparison, and ceiling

The action bundle removes the quadratic Gaussian-component chart when only
`R` actual source columns must remain resident: it uses `2*q*R` resident field
cells and a conservative `4*q*R` current-plus-output-buffer peak. Exact
invertibility preserves all declared `R` directions. Explicitly carrying the
entire two-fiber basis as columns uses `4*q^2` field cells. This is an
accounting identity for that explicit bundle, not a lower bound against
nonlinear representations or the complete public program descriptor.

The strongest executed final-boundary comparison streams each required source
through the identical public operator word in `2*q` dynamic field cells, plus
the counted `12*depth` public plan. The public word is itself a complete
rematerialization descriptor because this experiment has no hidden dynamic
operator parameter. Thus the result calibrates the cost of resident open
action state; it does not establish compact general open-relation closure or a
phase resource unavailable to classical software.

The exact ceiling is the nine declared safe-prime pairs, the two public
families, primary depth at most 4, alternate depth 3, source ranks 10 or 22,
the declared basis-column carrier and four-probe boundary, and direct-process
Linux software. The package does not establish full-source execution at every
q, a nonlinear-representation lower bound, CATVM custody, a distinct phase
resource, computational advantage, a Small Wall crossing, physical waveform
execution, replacement of physical bits with pi, or unbounded catalytic
computation.
