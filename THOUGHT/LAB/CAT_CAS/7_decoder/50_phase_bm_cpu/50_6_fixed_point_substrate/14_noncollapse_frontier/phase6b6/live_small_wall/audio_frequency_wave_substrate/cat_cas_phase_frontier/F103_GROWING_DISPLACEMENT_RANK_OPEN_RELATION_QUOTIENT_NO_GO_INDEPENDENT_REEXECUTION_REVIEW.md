The oracle passes 1,628 comparisons, including final chart commitments and

## Evidence boundary

The independent decision covers relation semantics, one-layer rank
saturation, exact chart encoding, restoration, mutations, and resident-size
formulas. Production's streamed field-operation totals, declared transient
scratch maxima, and Python/NumPy runtime exclusions remain package-local
accounting. Those fields support diagnosis but are not independently
recounted by this oracle.

The phase path does not construct an ordinary dense relation table, but its
rank-`n` displacement payload is the displacement transpose plus one boundary
row. Avoiding the entry-table layout therefore does not establish compact
state.
