## Mechanism

In characteristic 17, `t^17 - 1 = (t - 1)^17`. Therefore the cyclic group
algebra is exactly `F17[epsilon]/(epsilon^17)` with `t = 1 + epsilon`, and
reduction modulo `epsilon^rank` is an algebra quotient for every rank at most
17. M161 executes rank-2, rank-4, and rank-8 resident Hasse jets directly.
Translation-invariant weighted open-relation composition becomes truncated
jet multiplication. A reversible nonlinear module adds the convolution square
of the unresolved A relation to B, interleaved with public phase shifts,
invertible public relation kernels, and a noncommuting linear shear.

Production covers two public module orders at depths 1, 4, 16, and 64. All 24
final-only scalar B boundaries and complete final jet commitments match the
executed identical rank-r classical truncated-polynomial recurrence. Actual
reverse operations restore the exact borrowed jet cells on the same backing.
An alternate-family program then consumes the carrier restored from a primary
program, reaches restoration generation two, and matches a fresh carrier
without snapshot reload or retained inverse history.

## Independent reconstruction

The independent oracle imports neither production nor NumPy. It executes the
full 17-coefficient cyclic group algebra, including full cyclic convolution,
the nonlinear convolution-square shear, public kernel conversion, and exact
inverse. Only after the forward computation does it map the full carrier to
the declared Hasse-jet quotient. It matches 96 production fields over all 24
cases and independently restores the full 34-cell semantic-reference carrier.

Fifty-four additional exact checks show that addition, rotation, and cyclic
convolution descend to the rank-2, rank-4, and rank-8 jets. Explicit
`epsilon^rank` witnesses confirm that higher Hasse coordinates are discarded.
The same witnesses show that coefficientwise Hadamard intersection is not
well-defined on this quotient, so no general open-relation intersection claim
is accepted.

## Controls and resource law

Missing, wrong, and reordered inverses fail restoration for the tested
noncommuting program. Premature boundary projection and A-port projection are
rejected. Null, wrong-rank, disabled-carrier, scalar-mass-only, and
highest-retained-coordinate perturbations behave as required.

At rank 8 the accepted carrier has 16 F17 cells, logical transient accounting
peaks at 56 cells, and inverse history is zero. The full 34-cell group algebra
exists only in the independent semantic oracle. The strongest matched compact
classical method is the executed identical 16-cell jet recurrence with the
same 56-cell conservative logical working peak and the same field-operation
law, so both declared ratios are exactly one. Python allocator and
whole-process peak bytes were not measured.

## Claim ceiling

The result is limited to translation-invariant C17 weighted relations over
F17, ranks 2/4/8, two declared program families, depths through 64, and final
linear B-jet boundaries. It does not preserve higher Hasse moments, general
Hadamard intersection, non-translation-invariant relations, or arbitrary
graphs. It establishes no CATVM custody, distinct phase resource,
computational advantage, Small Wall crossing, physical execution, physical
