- forward and reverse topology, controls, and public-program fingerprints;
- byte expansion and SHA-256 reconstruction of every 867-byte final carrier;
- unrelated depth-1537 reuse and 100 depth-64 reuse cycles; and
- the rejected unrenormalized complex-coordinate diagnostic.

All 21 final carrier commitments and boundaries were reconstructed. The mask
and residue states agree exactly, every inverse returned to the original state,
and all 323 oracle comparisons passed.

## Mechanism and controls

One resident C17 phase factor controls 16 target triplets by native group
convolution. The updated target phase factors then control reciprocal hub
updates. OUT/IN order changes the boundary. The accepted production path does
not decode a resident phase factor to an exponent or angle, and it exposes only
the final cyclotomic boundary.

Missing, wrong, and reordered inverses leave the carrier changed. Disabling the
resident port, mutating the public hub topology, or changing OUT/IN order changes
the result. Premature boundary projection, resident-port projection, wrong
program ownership, and a null carrier are rejected. A gauge-only port mutation
changes the final boundary, and both tested carriers restore exactly.

The earlier raw complex-coordinate version is not accepted. Its maximum unit
norm error crosses the predeclared `1e-8` threshold at depth 64. No normalization
was inserted because doing so would require a different restoration
classification.

## Resource law and obstruction

The accepted carrier stores 51 logical phase factors as 867 one-byte orbit
coordinates. Its declared forward step performs 179 logical cyclic
convolutions, counted as 51,731 coordinate multiplications. The strongest
executed comparison stores the same state in 51 one-byte residues and performs
the corresponding 179 modular additions. It produces the identical expanded
state and final boundary in every case.

Thus the direct phase port and exact convolution are real software mechanisms,
but this pure one-hot C17 orbit has an exact 17-times-smaller compact classical
quotient. The result establishes neither a distinct phase resource nor a
computational advantage.

## Claim boundary

Preserved:

- bounded direct resident phase-port convolution;
- multiple noncommuting consumers;
- final-only boundary projection;
- exact same-backing restoration and unrelated reuse; and
- absence of relation tables, assignment expansion, and retained inverse plans.

Not established:

- coherent superposition over multiple phase-orbit coordinates;
- general relational contraction or machine-enforced CATVM custody;
- a resource beyond the 51-residue recurrence;
- computational advantage or Small Wall crossing;
- physical waveform execution or physical bit replacement; or
- unbounded catalytic computation.
