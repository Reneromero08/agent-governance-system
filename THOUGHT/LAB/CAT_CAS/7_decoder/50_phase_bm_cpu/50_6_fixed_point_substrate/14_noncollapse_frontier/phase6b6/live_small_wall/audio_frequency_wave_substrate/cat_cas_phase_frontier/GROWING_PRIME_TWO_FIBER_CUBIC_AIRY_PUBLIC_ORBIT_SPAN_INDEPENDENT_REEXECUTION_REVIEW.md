# Growing-Prime Two-Fiber Cubic Airy Public-Orbit Review

## Decision

`INDEPENDENTLY_VERIFIED_STRICT_SCOPE`

Verification level: `INDEPENDENT_ORACLE_REEXECUTION`

Restoration class: `EXACT_ALGEBRAIC_RESTORATION` for the discrete factor
payload, public morphism stack, stage, and same graph backing. Temporary Airy
tables, rank blocks, dense sample states, and independent full-orbit matrices
have `NO_RESTORATION_CLAIM`.

## Reconstructed mechanism

The experiment varies two public controlled-cubic coefficients around the
declared depth-one Gaussian operation order. The initial exact carrier has one
q-cell latent factor and two q-cell data fibers. For the primary order, the
first controlled cubic followed by the data Gaussian transform produces two
q-by-q cubic Airy tables. Fourier decomposition over the second public
coefficient partitions the public orbit by the level sets of `z=s*x^3`.

Every declared safe-prime order has `q = 2 mod 3`, so cubing permutes the field.
The growing rank calculation needs only the zero level and one representative
nonzero level, not a q-squared by q-squared orbit matrix. Both primary blocks
have rank q, hence the primary public orbit has exact rank q-squared. In the
alternate order, the independently reconstructed latent Airy table is
exhaustively certified as one quadratic phase chart. Nonzero levels are
related by exact row and column phase scalings; the zero block has rank q and
each nonzero block has rank q-minus-one for every declared q at least 11.

This is a lower bound for regular exact linear quotients on these fixed source
orbits. It is not a lower bound for nonlinear, singular, procedural, or
program-specific factor representations.

## Independent attacks and results

- The no-import oracle separately rebuilds safe-prime fields, qth roots,
  fixtures, Gaussian kernels, primary and alternate operation orders, Airy
  tables, level blocks, finite-field elimination, dense sampled execution,
  graph inverses, and reuse.
- Primary orbit rank is exactly q-squared at q=5, 11, 23, 29, 41, 53, 83, 89,
  and 113. The largest certified span is 12,769.
- Alternate orbit rank is exactly q-squared-minus-q-plus-one at q=11, 23, 29,
  41, 53, 83, 89, and 113. The largest certified span is 12,657.
- The oracle calculates every level rank independently at every declared
  order. It also materializes the full public-orbit matrices at q=5 and q=11;
  their direct ranks are 25, 121, and 111 for primary q=5, primary q=11, and
  alternate q=11 respectively.
- All 51 sampled final dense boundaries and state digests match independent
  reconstruction: three public coefficient pairs for each of 17 growing
  cases.
- Removing the second public shear leaves at most q source states. One data
  fiber alone does not produce the primary full q-squared span, and duplicating
  the second fiber also fails to do so. These controls show that both public
  shears and the distinct second data fiber are active in the accepted result.
- Missing, wrong, and reordered inverse controls fail. The primary graph is
  restored, the actual same graph backing is then consumed by the alternate
  program, generation reaches two, the result matches fresh execution, and no
  snapshot is used.

## Resource law and matched baseline

The resident input factor payload is `3*q` field elements plus five public
morphism records. The primary rank diagnostic temporarily constructs `2*q^2`
Airy entries and at most `(4*q-2)*q` rank-block entries. The growing path never
materializes a q-squared by q-squared public-orbit matrix. The oracle's q=5 and
q=11 full matrices are explicit verification baselines, not accepted carrier
state.

There is no resource advantage. Classical software executes the identical
`3*q` factor graph and the identical block-rank calculation. It can also use
the identical q-squared dense joint recurrence. The result rejects a smaller
linear evaluation quotient while preserving the compact procedural factor
representation; it does not turn the span bound into a universal storage
lower bound.

## Claim ceiling

The ceiling is the nine declared primary safe-prime pairs, the eight declared
alternate pairs with q at least 11, depth one, both declared data fibers, the
fixed Gaussian fixtures and operation orders, the complete public pair of
controlled-cubic coefficients, exact finite-field software, and regular
linear quotients of these source orbits.

Arbitrary source families, nonlinear or singular message quotients, a
universal subquadratic-state lower bound, polynomial-work subquadratic-state
closure, CATVM custody, a distinct phase resource, computational advantage,
Small Wall crossing, physical waveform or silicon execution, replacement of
physical bits with pi, and unbounded catalytic computation remain
unestablished.
