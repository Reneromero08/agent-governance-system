# M255 focused independent review

Disposition: `PASS_STRICT_SCOPE`

The review inspected the production compact cumulant carrier, standalone full
exterior oracle, qualifier, and all three sealed outputs.  It also checked the
post-review rank-certificate repair against the exact descriptor supports.

Verified strict-scope results:

- Native intersection adds the declared decomposable quartic cumulants to the
  actual compact logarithmic carrier without materializing the full even
  exterior signature.
- The independent oracle reconstructs exterior exponential, signed Hodge
  complement, normalization, and exterior logarithm.  It reproduces exact top
  connected cumulants `1/4`, `-2/27`, and `14/625` at 4, 6, and 8 ports.
- At six ports the oracle accumulates the actual two quartic supports, Hodge
  dualizes them, and squares the resulting two-form.  Support `[0,1,4,5]` has
  coefficient `2`.  This gives rank at least two; the displayed sum of two
  decomposable terms gives rank at most two, so the exact rank is two.
- Four-port Pluecker applicability is explicitly false.  Its scalar Hodge-dual
  square is not misclassified as a nondecomposability witness.
- Exact subtractive inverse execution restores the actual pair/quartic
  backings, generation-2 descriptor-distinct reuse agrees with a fresh
  carrier, and no baseline reload occurs.
- The qualifier pins the O(1) fixed-fixture certificates, the independent
  two-state continuant for the normalization scalar, and the separate
  descriptor-level streamed Pfaffian/set-partition comparator without claiming
  comparator optimality.
- A deterministic randomized check compared the compact selected projection
  with the standalone full-exterior oracle for 463 nonsingular public programs
  over the declared 4/6/8-port chain grammar.

Claim ceiling retained: this is a bounded exact rational counterexample inside
`Q(zeta8)`.  It rejects the rank-one quartic fixed-degree chart, but establishes
no general rank theorem, compact wide-interface closure, CATVM custody,
distinct phase resource, computational advantage, Small Wall crossing,
physical execution, inference, or unbounded catalytic computation.
