coupler, seal, expected boundary, or compact answer table. It can request
only an atomic primary or reuse transaction. The service holds the four
complex wave cells and verification seal in a locked, `MADV_DONTDUMP`,

one peer, removes its socket path, and installs a default-kill seccomp
filter.
