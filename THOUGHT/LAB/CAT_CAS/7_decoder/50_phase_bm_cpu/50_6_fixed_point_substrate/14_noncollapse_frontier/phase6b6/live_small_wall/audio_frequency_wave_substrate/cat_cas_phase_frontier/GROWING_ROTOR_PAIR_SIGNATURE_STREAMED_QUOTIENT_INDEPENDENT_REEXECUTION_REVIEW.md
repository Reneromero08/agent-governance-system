# M194 independent reexecution review

## Decision

`INDEPENDENTLY_VERIFIED_STRICT_SCOPE`

Verification level: `INDEPENDENT_ORACLE_REEXECUTION`

Restoration class: `EXACT_ALGEBRAIC_RESTORATION`

The bounded direct-process package eliminates the sixteen retained sparse
shift bases from the accepted pair-signature quotient runtime.  It does not
eliminate public topology material: the accepted path retains nine signature
coordinates and one 17-bin representative per fiber, plus one field-valued
boundary weight.  Each scattering step rematerializes signed pair moves.

## Independent construction

The production implementation streams weak compositions and uses a four-site
population delta to update the nine pair counts.  The oracle does not import
that source.  It independently:

1. enumerates bosonic occupations with
   `itertools.combinations_with_replacement` and sorts them into the declared
   public order;
2. reconstructs global-rotation representatives and pair-signature fibers;
3. enumerates ordered particle pairs rather than mode multiplicities;
4. constructs verification-only sparse rows and proves every necklace in a
   fiber has the same row for all sixteen signed shifts;
5. checks an ordered-particle row-local stream against those independently
   materialized rows; and
6. reconstructs all final commitments, boundaries, controls, restoration, and
   reuse values without calling production projection or inverse functions.

All production/oracle case fields agree exactly for rotor counts 2 through 5
over F103 and F239.  Production outputs also agree exactly with the sealed M193
quotient outputs and boundaries.

## Observed resource law

The quotient carrier remains 9, 33, 165, and 621 field cells, with two carrier
registers and two word-scratch vectors.  Retained shift plans and retained plan
nonzeros are both zero.

At five rotors, the removed M193 plan contained 131,168 nonzeros.  M194 retains
5,589 signature-descriptor integers, 10,557 representative-descriptor
integers, and 621 boundary field cells.  Thus the public integer descriptor is
8.12 times smaller than the prior nonzero count; including boundary weights,
the logical count ratio is 7.82.  This is not a byte-level or allocator claim.

One five-rotor scattering rematerializes 137,232 nonzero mode-pair-shift terms
(198,720 ordered-particle terms), 1.046 times the old plan nonzero count.  The
public compiler visits 20,349 occupations while keeping one live histogram.
The equitability verifier separately visits the full topology and peaks at 632
sparse row entries; those rows are verification-only and excluded from the
accepted path.  Python containers, allocator state, bigint representation,
expression temporaries, and whole-process peak memory remain unmeasured and
are not asserted zero.

The strongest matched compact classical method is the identical streamed
pair-signature recurrence, including the same descriptors and rematerialized
work.  No memory, work, or time advantage follows.

## Controls and restoration

The accepted two-register shear projects only the final scalar boundary,
recomputes the public word, subtracts it from the same target backing, verifies
exact discrete equality, and reuses the same source and target backing under a
different depth-five program.  Generation reaches two without snapshot or
baseline reload.  Missing, wrong, and reordered inverse controls leave
nonzero residual cells in every case; null carriers are rejected.

This is exact algebraic restoration of the shear.  It is not a claim that the
generally noninvertible scattering word is itself inverted.

## Claim ceiling

The result is limited to Grid17, exchange-symmetric, global-rotation-invariant,
pair-signature-fiber-constant inputs at rotor counts 2 through 5, over F103 and
F239, with primary depth 8 and reuse depth 5 in direct-process software.

Preserved subclaims are exact fiber closure, diagonal two-body phase closure,
zero retained inverse history, final-only scalar projection, exact same-backing
restoration, unrelated reuse, and zero retained shift bases.

Rejected interpretations include arbitrary necklace inputs, fixed-rank
growth, zero topology cost, zero rematerialization cost, CATVM custody,
machine-enforced no-smuggle behavior, a distinct phase resource,
computational advantage, Small Wall crossing, physical waveform execution,
physical replacement of bits with pi, and unbounded computation.

## Next obstruction

Plan residency is removed, but the public topology still grows as 26 integer
descriptor cells per fiber and every scattering rematerializes a growing term
stream.  The next mechanism should derive the scattering action from the
pair-signature coordinates themselves, or establish that no descriptor-free
analytic kernel exists, while retaining the identical classical recurrence as
the matched baseline.
