# F103 C17 Paley association phase-relation closure independent reexecution review

## Decision

`INDEPENDENTLY_VERIFIED_STRICT_SCOPE`

Verification level: `INDEPENDENT_ORACLE_REEXECUTION`

Restoration class: `EXACT_ALGEBRAIC_RESTORATION`

## Mechanism

The carrier restricts C17 translation-invariant relation kernels to functions
constant on three difference classes: zero, the eight nonzero quadratic
residues, and the eight quadratic nonresidues. This is an automorphism-orbit
subalgebra, not a quotient representing arbitrary relations. Pointwise
Hadamard intersection is componentwise multiplication in the three class
values. Cyclic relation composition is the exact three-coordinate product
defined by the Paley intersection numbers.

Production retains two unresolved three-coordinate relation ports. Each step
has two consumers of each port and interleaves nonlinear relation squares,
public kernels, public masks, composition shears, and intersection shears in
two noncommuting public orders. Twelve cases cover depths 1, 4, 16, 64, 256,
and 1024. Only the final scalar B boundary is projected; A is never
serialized.

## Independent reconstruction

The oracle imports neither production nor NumPy. It reconstructs quadratic
residues and nonresidues directly, enumerates all full C17 sums, and obtains
the composition tensor

```text
E0*E0 = E0       E0*Q = Q          E0*N = N
Q*Q   = 8E0+3Q+4N
Q*N   = 4Q+4N
N*N   = 8E0+4Q+3N
```

All nine basis compositions and all nine basis Hadamard products remain class
constant. A delta supported at one residue is independently rejected as
outside the family. The oracle then executes both registers as complete
17-coordinate relations. It matches 60 production fields across all 12 cases
and restores all 34 semantic-reference cells exactly.

## Restoration, controls, and resources

Production reverses the actual forward shears, restores the six borrowed
field cells on the same backing, and reuses that carrier for an unrelated
alternate-family program. The second execution matches a fresh carrier and
reaches restoration generation two without snapshot reload or retained
inverse history. Direct Python backing identity remains package-local.

Missing, wrong, and reordered inverses fail restoration. Premature and hidden
A projections are rejected, as are null carriers and undeclared depths. A
disabled carrier and an invalid merge of the residue and nonresidue classes
change the boundary. The F103 scalar 72 has exact multiplicative order 17.

The accepted two-register carrier has six F103 cells, at most six public
kernel/mask cells per step, and a conservative 15-cell logical working peak.
The strongest matched classical implementation is separately executed and is
the identical six-carrier-cell, 15-working-cell recurrence. The full
34-coordinate relations exist only in the independent oracle. No dense
17-by-17 table or inverse history is retained. Whole-process and Python
allocator peaks were not measured.

## Claim ceiling

The result is limited to C17 translation-invariant F103 relations constant on
the zero/residue/nonresidue classes, two declared program orders, and depths
through 1024. It does not cover general C17 relations, non-translation-
invariant relations, arbitrary graphs, or a general association-scheme
compiler. It establishes no CATVM custody, distinct phase resource,
computational advantage, Small Wall crossing, physical waveform or silicon
execution, replacement of physical bits with pi, or unbounded catalytic
computation.
