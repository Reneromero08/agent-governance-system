# M235 focused independent review

Disposition: `PASS_STRICT_SCOPE`

The review inspected the production source, standalone sparse-dictionary
reference, sealed outputs, and qualifier.  It reconstructed the cubic
critical-locus law from
`d/dw [w^3/3-(v+c)w] = w^2-v-c`, checked the exact substitution and inverse,
and checked the square-free induction used to equate polynomial degree with
the number of distinct complex branches for every declared case.

An initial public description incorrectly called multiplication by `(v-h)`
relation intersection.  For zero-locus semantics that product adjoins the
branch `v=h`; it is a union, not set-theoretic intersection.  Before sealing,
the package was repaired to call the operation public linear branch product
or branch union everywhere.  The result and qualifier explicitly record that
set-theoretic relation intersection is not established.

The final review found that:

- the two independent representations agree on all 16 cases, controls,
  commitments, degree/payload signatures, restoration, and reuse;
- exact preflights establish simple branches without enumerating roots;
- final projection is rejected before the full public word, and the shared
  stationary coordinate cannot be projected;
- the actual coefficient container restores exactly, generation advances
  from one to two, the same container is reused by the alternate family, and
  no baseline reload occurs;
- missing, wrong, and reordered inverse paths fail release;
- the public descriptor expression DAG exactly preserves the open relation
  in `O(depth)` descriptors, while its stronger implemented scalar evaluator
  reproduces the final boundary with two persistent integers;
- resource figures remain `PACKAGE_SELF_REVIEW` and do not claim complete
  process liveness or integer bit-operation cost.

The accepted scope is limited to the declared formal cubic critical-locus
family through depth eight.  It does not establish general cubic relation
closure, set-theoretic intersection, CATVM custody, a distinct phase resource,
computational advantage, Small Wall crossing, physical waveform execution,
physical-bit replacement, inference, or unbounded computation.
